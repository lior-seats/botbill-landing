Frequently asked questions

Won't blocking AI bots hurt my SEO?
No. BotBill is specifically designed to block AI training and retrieval crawlers — not search engine indexers. Googlebot, Bingbot, and other search crawlers are never touched. You can review every rule before it's applied.
Which hosting platforms are supported?
At launch: Vercel Log Drains, Cloudflare Logpush, and Nginx access logs. AWS CloudFront and Fly.io are on the roadmap. If you use something else, let us know — we build based on waitlist demand.
How is this different from Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode?
Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode costs $20/month (Pro plan), gives you no visibility into which specific AI crawlers are hitting you, and applies a one-size-fits-all challenge. BotBill shows you per-bot cost attribution and lets you make surgical decisions — block Meta's crawler, allow OpenAI's, rate-limit Perplexity — without guessing.
Do I need to change my code or DNS?
No code changes required. You connect BotBill to your existing log source (a one-time token or endpoint configuration). For blocking, BotBill generates the rules and you paste them into your Cloudflare dashboard or vercel.json — it takes about 60 seconds.
